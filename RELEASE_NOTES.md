AFewBuds Mobile Beta 0.16.0-mobile-beta.5

This release carries forward the complete public beta.4 game, UI, account sessions, cloud-save format and updater. Improvements were developed and regression-tested in afewbuds-cloud-test.

- Furniture remains free-movement, with actual-model previews, walk-around placement, edited layouts, wall-mounted stash options and safer property boundaries.
- Apartment and purchased house keep separate furniture, grow tents, station inventory, product stock, paid utility bills and employee assignments.
- The house computer now shows the house's own staff, storage, business, equipment and bills instead of apartment staff and storefront details. Workers transfer only when assigned; purchases and stored stock stay in their original property.
- House grow room panel now detects placed tents, planted crops, light usage and separate installed ventilation. Placing or picking up equipment updates the display and power usage. Ventilation improves house crops only while a house unit is installed and running.
- Repaired NPC couch-position tracking and sit poses for Kobi, Malik and Rod, with normal standing idle poses on arrival at production stations.
- Restored original scissors and drag-to-bag workflows: Bench I uses 3g bags; Bench II and III trim larger amounts and seal 7g bags repeatedly with a final remainder.
- Corrected placed-bench bud and weight displays, removed abandoned original station labels, and allowed more precise wall-adjacent furniture placement.
- Preserves the original career, progression, contacts, genetics, phones, and property purchase systems.

Regression coverage includes property isolation, worker and NPC navigation, house grow/ventilation, packing, cloud-save migration, and core gameplay.
