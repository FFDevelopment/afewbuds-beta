# AFewBuds 0.16.0 beta.3

- New houses start empty; apartment belongings, stock and workers stay at the apartment.
- Order equipment to a property curb or buy to Backpack and place it in either owned property.
- Real Estate lists both properties with activity status and property-specific furniture controls.
- Pickup, movement and placement require being inside the selected property.
- Computers use the inventory of the property where they are placed.
- Existing saved furniture and upgrades are preserved; stocked equipment still cannot be picked up.
