AFewBuds 0.7.9-beta.20

- Godot now sends the exact career JSON to the account cloud bridge after loading a local career and after every successful save.
- Cloud uploads are serialized so rapid saves cannot arrive out of order.
- If cloud upload fails, the local Godot save remains untouched and gameplay continues.
- Existing browser-to-Home-Screen restore logic and the transactional auto-updater remain enabled.
